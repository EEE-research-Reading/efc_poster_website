Building Flexibility for a Net-Zero Grid

Making building energy flexibility visible, measurable, and easy to compare

Most people are familiar with Energy Performance Certificates (EPCs), which summarise how energy-efficient a building is. Energy Flexibility Certificates (EFCs) apply the same clear idea to electricity flexibility: showing how much—and how easily—a building can shift its power use to cheaper, lower-carbon times of day.

Illustrative Prototype

What could an Energy Flexibility Certificate look like in practice?

Borrowing the familiar, easy-to-scan layout of a UK Energy Performance Certificate, this mock Demand Flexibility Certificate shows how complex building physics and smart-tech data can be distilled into practical guidance for property owners, tenants, and grid planners.

Summer C4 Winter A3

Separate Summer & Winter Ratings

Heating, cooling, and occupancy patterns shift markedly across the year, so every building receives a paired seasonal rating rather than a single year-round average.

A–F · 1–6

Dual Score: How Much & How Easily

Letters (A–F) show the Nominal size of demand that can be shifted, whilst numbers (1–6) show the Quality—how quickly, frequently, and reliably the building can respond.

Tech

At-a-Glance Technical Profile & Benchmarks

Highlights key flexible equipment (such as an automated heat pump) alongside clear benchmarks comparing the property against newly built (A1) and typical existing buildings (D5).
